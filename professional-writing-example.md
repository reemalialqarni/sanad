# Professional Writing Workflow

## Scenario

Draft interface content for notifying Noura that Sanad has detected a possible match. The message must reduce anxiety without creating false certainty.

## 1. AI-Generated Draft

> Great news! We found your lost bag. Verify your identity now to claim it.

## 2. Fact Verification

| Claim or element | Verification result | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "We found your lost bag" | Unsupported. The system identified only a possible match. | Replace with accurate uncertainty language. |
| "Verify your identity" | Incomplete. The step verifies ownership details; identity verification policy is not specified. | Refer to ownership questions, not identity verification. |
| "Claim it" | Premature. An employee must review the evidence before release. | Explain the employee-review step. |
| Timing or collection details | Not provided. | Do not invent them. |

## 3. Tone Refinement

The tone was changed from celebratory and certain to calm, precise, and reassuring. The revised message avoids implying success before verification and explains why the next step is necessary.

### Refined version

> We found a report with details similar to your lost item. This is not a confirmed match yet. Answer a few private questions, and an airport employee will review the result.

## 4. Human Review and Sign-Off

- The message accurately distinguishes a **possible match** from a confirmed recovery.
- It does not reveal details of the found item.
- It tells the user what happens next.
- It preserves employee review before item release.
- No unconfirmed time estimate, policy, or outcome is stated.

**Human reviewer decision:** Approved for the conceptual prototype.

## 5. Final Version

### Possible match found

> We found a report with details similar to your lost item. Answer a few private questions so an airport employee can review the match.

**Primary action:** Continue verification  
**Secondary action:** Review my report

## Why the Workflow Matters

Publishing the initial draft would have created false confidence and misrepresented the service process. Verification and human sign-off changed not only the tone but also the factual accuracy and risk level of the message.
